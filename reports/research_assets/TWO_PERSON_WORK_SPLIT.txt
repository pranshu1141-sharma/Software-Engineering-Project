PERSON 2 - QUEUE PREDICTION, MODEL INTEGRATION AND VERIFICATION

3. Compare predicted wait errors with the simple arithmetic baseline.
4. Replace synthetic queue sessions with approved operational records later.
5. Connect saved models to FastAPI, define request/response formats, and test them.
6. Verify input handling, held-out splits, saved-model reload and demo behavior.
7. Lead future integration with booking, live queue, website and mobile clients.
